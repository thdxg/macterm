# Quick terminal

A global terminal accessible from anywhere with a hotkey — <kbd>⌃`</kbd> by default. It drops down over your current space, then gets out of your way when you're done. The quick terminal is scratch space: its sessions end on every quit, and its size and hotkey are configurable in **Macterm → Settings**.

The panel is centered by default. To move it, enable **Allow moving the panel** in **Settings → Quick Terminal**: a grab handle appears along the panel's top edge, and dragging it moves the panel like a title bar would. The position is remembered, so the panel reopens where you left it — even tucked partly off a screen edge. If the screen changed underneath it, the panel nudges back only as far as it takes to stay grabbable.
