OVERVIEW: Inspect and kill terminal sessions that zmx runs.

USAGE: macterm session <subcommand>

OPTIONS:
  -h, --help              Show help information.

SUBCOMMANDS:
  list (default)          List live zmx sessions.
  info                    Show one zmx session.
  kill                    Kill a zmx session. Its shell ends, and the shell of
                          a connected pane exits.

  See 'macterm help session <subcommand>' for detailed help.
