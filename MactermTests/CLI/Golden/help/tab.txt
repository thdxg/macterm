OVERVIEW: List, create, select, reorder, and close tabs.

USAGE: macterm tab <subcommand>

OPTIONS:
  -h, --help              Show help information.

SUBCOMMANDS:
  list (default)          List tabs. The default is the active project.
  new                     Open a new tab. It becomes active, unless you use
                          --no-focus.
  select                  Make a tab active.
  move                    Move a tab to a slot. The slot is the final position
                          of the tab in the `tab list` order, counted from 1.
  rename                  Rename a tab, or use --reset to restore its automatic
                          title.
  close                   Close a tab. This kills the zmx sessions of its panes.
  merge                   [debug] Merge a tab into the active tab, next to a
                          pane (--dest) or at the edge of the workspace.

  See 'macterm help tab <subcommand>' for detailed help.
