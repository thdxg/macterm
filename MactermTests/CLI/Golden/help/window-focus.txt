OVERVIEW: Bring a window to the front. It becomes the key window.

USAGE: macterm window focus <window> [--socket <socket>] [--json]

ARGUMENTS:
  <window>                Window to focus (index or id).

OPTIONS:
  --socket <socket>       Control socket path. It overrides discovery.
  --json                  Print the raw JSON payload instead of a table.
  -h, --help              Show help information.
