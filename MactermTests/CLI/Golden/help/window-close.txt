OVERVIEW: Close a window. The last visible window hides instead.

USAGE: macterm window close [--window <window>] [--socket <socket>] [--json]

OPTIONS:
  --window <window>       Window to close (index or id). The default is the
                          focused window.
  --socket <socket>       Control socket path. It overrides discovery.
  --json                  Print the raw JSON payload instead of a table.
  -h, --help              Show help information.
