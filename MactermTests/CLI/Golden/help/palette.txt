OVERVIEW: List the palettes of the installed extensions and show if Macterm can
read each one.

USAGE: macterm palette <subcommand>

OPTIONS:
  -h, --help              Show help information.

SUBCOMMANDS:
  list (default)          List the palettes of the extensions in
                          ~/.config/macterm/extensions with their name, state
                          and any error.

  See 'macterm help palette <subcommand>' for detailed help.
