What's New

  • On macOS 26 and later, the Dock icon and the app-switcher icon use the colors of your terminal theme, with real Liquid Glass. To turn this off, go to Settings → Appearance → App Icon. (#628, #634)
  • The app icon is new: glass panes over a grid of terminal characters. (#628, #638)
  • A file that opens with Macterm shows a Macterm document icon in Finder. (#638)
  • Each card in Settings → Extensions shows the screenshots of its extension. Click a screenshot to see it at full size. (#635)
  • eyesclosed is the default theme. (#627)

Notes

  • If your config has no theme line, the terminal colors change from Rose Pine to eyesclosed. To keep Rose Pine, add theme = "Rose Pine" to your config. (#627)
