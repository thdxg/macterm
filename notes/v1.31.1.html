Fixes

  • Macterm no longer hangs at launch with two windows open on the same tab, or when closing a pane that is flooding the terminal with title updates. (#466)
  • Reattaching a session with a long, prompt-heavy scrollback no longer freezes the app while the history replays. (#465)
  • Desktop widgets now move with your displays like the system's own widgets, returning to where you put them on each display instead of ending up off-screen after unplugging a monitor. (#461)
  • The Settings window can be dragged by its titlebar again after adjusting a slider. (#462)

Thanks to @thdxg for this release.
