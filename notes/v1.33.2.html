What's New

  • Settings → General → Opening Text Files has a Make Default… button that makes Macterm the default app of every text file type with one click. (#666)

Fixes

  • After you close a second window on a tab, keys such as ⌃C, ⌃D and the arrows reach the program in a new pane again. (#660)
  • In Settings, a pane that you scroll no longer shows through its title. (#668)
  • macterm session info no longer reports a client that does not exist. (#669)
  • Kitty graphics images are removed with the scrollback that you clear, and a cancelled image transfer leaves no partial data. (#664)

Thanks to @carlocaione and @thdxg for this release.
