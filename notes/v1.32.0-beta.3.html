What's New

  • Settings → Animations → Snap to whole row now scrolls a whole row at a time and slides each row into place, the way less does, so the view never comes to rest between rows. (#517)

Fixes

  • Snap to whole row now works with mice that scroll smoothly without trackpad gestures, such as a Logitech mouse with smooth scrolling on. (#517)

Notes

  • With Snap to whole row on, resizing a split moves its text a row at a time instead of sliding.

Thanks to @thdxg for this release.
