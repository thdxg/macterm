What's New

  • Settings is now a command. It is in the command palette, and you can change its keybind (⌘, by default) in Settings → Keymaps. (#649)
  • Each path in Settings → General → Ghostty Config is a link. Click it to open the file in your terminal editor. (#646)
  • The app icon changed again: four glass panes around a cross, over characters in the ANSI colors of the theme. On macOS 26 and later, the Dock icon paints the characters with the colors of your theme. (#640, #644)
  • The Macterm document icon in Finder is plainer: one glass pane over the characters. (#645)

Notes

  • The default window padding is now 12 points (before, it was 16). To keep the old padding, add window-padding-x = 16 and window-padding-y = 16 to your config. (#641)
  • Peek sidebar when hidden (Settings → Appearance → Sidebar) is now off by default. If you changed this setting before, Macterm keeps your value. (#641)
