What's New

  • Scratch tabs are tabs that belong to no project. Press ⌘⇧T, or right-click the empty space of the sidebar, to open one in your home folder. Scratch tabs show below the projects in the sidebar. When you close a scratch tab, it is gone. You can also move a project tab to the scratch tabs, pin a scratch tab, or open one with macterm tab new --project scratch. (#670)
  • The terminal core is updated to Ghostty 1.4.0. (#677)

Fixes

  • In the quick terminal, the Copy Mode and Read-Only Mode keybinds now change the pane of the quick terminal, not a pane behind the panel. (#675)

Notes

  • If you go back to v1.33 or earlier, that version does not restore your scratch tabs, and it ends their sessions.

Thanks to @thdxg for this release.
