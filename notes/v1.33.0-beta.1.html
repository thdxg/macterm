What's New

  • Copy mode lets you move a cursor through the scrollback with the keyboard and select text. Press ⌘⇧Space in a pane. The default keys are the keys of Vim. To use the keys of Helix, go to Settings → Keymaps → Copy Mode. A click moves the cursor, and the page motions scroll smoothly. (#608, #611, #613, #614, #615, #618, #624)
  • Explain Output: Apple Intelligence on your Mac explains the output of the last command, or the selected text, in a popover. Press ⌘⇧E, or right-click in a pane. The text of the pane does not leave your Mac. It needs macOS 26 and a Mac that supports Apple Intelligence. (#622)
  • Read-Only Mode stops all input to a pane, and a Read-Only pill shows that the mode is on. The command is in the command palette and in Settings → Keymaps. (#612)
  • Macterm is available in Korean and Simplified Chinese. Settings → General → Language selects the language, or System. (#617, #619, #620)
  • Each category in Settings → Keymaps folds, and the keybinds of extensions are in their own Extensions group. (#625)
  • All the badges of a pane (Read-Only, Copy Mode, Zoomed and the secure-input lock) are in one row in the top-right corner of the pane. The zoomed pane of the quick terminal now shows the Zoomed pill too. (#609)

Fixes

  • Ctrl+L at a shell prompt keeps the cleared screen in the scrollback again. Before, the text was lost when the screen was not full. (#610)

Notes

  • Snap to whole row (Settings → Animations) is now on by default. If you changed this setting before, Macterm keeps your value. (#623)
