What's New

  • Move Tab Up and Move Tab Down move the active tab in the sidebar. Move Pane Left, Right, Up and Down swap the focused pane with its neighbor. Each one has a default keybind that you can change in Settings → Keymaps. (#600)
  • A tab that runs omp shows the omp logo in the sidebar. (#601)
  • A full-screen program such as Helix or vim keeps a translucent window translucent, because painted cell backgrounds now follow the window opacity. (#606)
  • On macOS 26, the Zoomed pill is a glass button, and a zoom of any pane slides the other panes out of the window. (#605)

Notes

  • To get the opaque cell backgrounds of Ghostty again, set background-opacity-cells = false in your config.
